My doctoral research investigates sound field control with loudspeaker arrays, including personal sound zones and transaural reproduction. I study how errors in estimated room acoustics affect reproduction and how neural-network-based filter design compares with conventional optimisation.

A related direction uses neural interpolation of BRIRs from sparse measurements to support control at unmeasured positions.
