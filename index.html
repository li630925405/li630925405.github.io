About me

I am a PhD researcher in Artificial Intelligence and Music at the Centre for Digital Music (C4DM), Queen Mary University of London, supervised by Joshua Reiss and Lin Wang.

My research connects spatial acoustic modelling and sound field control. I develop neural representations of binaural room impulse responses (BRIRs) from sparse measurements and study how these models can support personal sound zones and loudspeaker reproduction.

In 2026, I was a research intern at the National Institute of Informatics, working with Shoichi Koyama on HRTF-conditioned BRIR personalisation. Previously, I worked on HRTF personalisation at Peking University with Tianshu Qu and received my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Beihang University.

Research interests

  • Neural acoustic modelling — Reconstructing binaural room impulse responses from sparse spatial measurements using SIREN and physics-informed networks.
  • Personal sound zones — Learning filter designs for loudspeaker arrays, with an emphasis on robustness to acoustic modelling errors.
  • Personalised spatial audio — Conditioning room acoustic models on individual HRTFs to account for differences between listeners.
